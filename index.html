FAQ

Questions?

The ones we get most often.

Can I book travel, hotels, and movies in a single checkout?
Yes! That's exactly what TravelHub is built for. Add travel tickets, hotel rooms, and movie tickets to a single booking and pay for everything in one transaction. No separate apps, no repeated payment steps.
Are the hotel prices on the platform guaranteed?
Yes. The prices shown are final and inclusive of all taxes and fees. If you find a lower price for the same room on the hotel's own website within 24 hours of booking, we'll match it. No questions asked.
How do I cancel or modify a booking?
Use the "My Bookings" section in the app. Travel and hotel bookings can typically be cancelled up to 24 hours before. Movie tickets can be cancelled up to 1 hour before the show. Policies vary by booking type.
Is my payment information secure?
Absolutely. We use 256-bit SSL encryption and are PCI-DSS compliant. We never store your card details on our servers. All payments go through RBI-approved payment gateways.
Which cities in India does TravelHub cover?
Travel and hotels are available across 200+ cities in India. Movie bookings are live in 50+ cities with rapid expansion underway. Sign up for early access and we'll notify you the moment we launch in your city.
How fast will I receive my booking confirmation?
Instantly. As soon as your payment is confirmed, you'll receive a booking confirmation via email and SMS within 30 seconds. Tickets are also available to download directly from the app.
